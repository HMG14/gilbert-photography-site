Product / Commercial

Commercial

Studio and location work for commercial clients, across construction, farming, and product photography.

For over two decades, I have been creating still and video images for clients across various sectors, including corporate, agriculture, retail, non-profit organizations, and magazines.

My first camera, a Nikon FTN, was a gift from my father, which he brought back from a business trip to Japan. It sits on a shelf near my desk and reminds me that compelling images are as much a function of the photographer’s eye as the camera platform they use.

I still shoot with Nikon cameras, as I appreciate their fluid interface and excellent optics.

Construction

Structural work on high-rise builds, shot from the deck alongside the ironworkers and concrete crews putting it up.

Agriculture

A different scale of work — grain elevators, wheat fields, and the equipment that keeps a working farm running.

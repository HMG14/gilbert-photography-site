About

Gilbert B Hammer

For over two decades, I have been creating still and video images for clients across corporate, agriculture, retail, non-profit and magazine work. My first camera was a Nikon FTN, a gift from my father, who brought it back from a business trip to Japan. I learned to shoot in high school, then worked for several years as an assistant to an accomplished professional photographer. I also attended the Film & Video Workshops in Rockport, Maine, where I honed my skills with lighting.

Today I shoot with a number of Nikon cameras, both DSLR and mirrorless, for their fluid interface and excellent optics. The FTN still works, many years after I first picked it up. It’s a reminder that compelling images are as much a function of the photographer’s eye as the camera platform they use.

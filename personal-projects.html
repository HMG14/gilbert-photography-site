New York City

A city I once called home, seen empty during the 2020 shutdown and quiet again from the harbor at dusk.

Walking around Manhattan during the pandemic was like visiting another planet, especially for someone who once lived there — empty streets, shuttered shops, and an eerily quiet Grand Central Terminal.

Crossing the Hudson River, the Space Shuttle Enterprise appeared in front of our ferry on its final trip to the U.S.S. Intrepid Museum.

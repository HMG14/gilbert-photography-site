Landscape / Street

Personal Projects

Landscapes, birds in flight, cityscapes — ongoing personal work.

These are the images I make without a client or a deadline. I go back to the same places more than once, and wait for the light, the weather, or the moment to be right before I raise the camera.

Walking around Manhattan during the pandemic was like visiting another planet, especially for someone who once lived there — empty streets, shuttered shops, and an eerily quiet Grand Central Terminal.
